Our mission

A token of HOPE to disadvantaged communities.

The organisation's stated mission is to respond timeously to requests for donations, help alleviate poverty, and provide basic needs to people who need support.

Its profile describes its vision as being a token of “HOPE” to disadvantaged communities in South Africa.
